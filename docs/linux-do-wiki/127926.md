# ZH

> [域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)

> 构成[统一资源定位符](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-zh) ([URL](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-zh))，使得人们能够在互联网（例如：网页、电子邮件服务器、图像和视频）上查找资源的一种唯一名称。[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)自身代表着互联网上的一个特定地址，该地址属于一个实体，例如：企业、组织、机构或个人。例如，在这个 [URL](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-zh) 中： **[https://www.icann.org/public-comments**，[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)](https://www.icann.org/public-comments**%EF%BC%8C%5B%E5%9F%9F%E5%90%8D%5D(https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)) **icann.org** 负责把浏览器定向到 [ICANN 组织](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/icann-organization-zh)的[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)。该 [URL](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-zh) 的其他部分则指示浏览器到 **www** 服务器中 [ICANN](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/internet-corporation-for-assigned-names-and-numbers-zh) [域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)之下查找一项特定资源（在这个例子中，您将登录到 [ICANN](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/internet-corporation-for-assigned-names-and-numbers-zh) 网站的[公共评议](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/public-comment-zh)期网页）。

> 一个[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)由两个或多个[字符串](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/string-zh)构成，中间用句点分隔开来。例如，在[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh) **icann.org** 中的第一部分 **icann** 代表着[顶级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh) **org** 中的一个[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh)。[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)还可以拥有超过两个以上的[字符串](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/string-zh)，例如：**bbc.co.uk**。在这个例子中，**bbc** 代表着[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh) **co** 中的一个[子域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-zh)，而后者则是[顶级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh) **uk** 中的一个[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh)。

> [标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)

> 在[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)中，各个部分以点[字符](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/character-zh)进行分隔。例如，[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh) **gnso.icann.org** 包含以下三个[标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)：**gnso**、**icann** 和 **org**。对于[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)中的每个[标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)，当从左到右读取时，每个[标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)依次代表紧跟该[标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)之后的[标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)的子[标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)。例如，在[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh) **gnso.icann.org** 的[标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)中，**gnso** 是 **icann** 域的[子域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-zh)，**icann** 是 **org** 域的[子域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-zh)。

> 在早期的[域名系统](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zh) ([DNS](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zh)) 中，[标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)只能由拉丁字母（a 到 z）、欧洲阿拉伯数字（0 到 9）和连[字符](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/character-zh) (-) 组成。现在，[DNS](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zh) 支持*[国际化域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/internationalized-domain-name-zh)*，它允许[标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)包含其他字母系统（例如，阿拉伯文和西里尔文）和语言（例如，中文、日文、韩文）中的[字符](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/character-zh)。

> [统一资源定位符(URL)](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-zh)

> [统一资源定位符](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-zh)是一个全球唯一的[字符](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/character-zh)序列，用于描述特定文件或资源（例如，流媒体视频、软件应用程序、在线服务）在互联网上的位置。[URL](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-zh) 还可以指明用于打开指定文件或资源的[协议](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/protocol-zh)。在 [URL](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-zh) [ICANN Lookup](https://whois.icann.org/en) 中，**https** 指明了用于打开位于 **ICANN Lookup** 的资源的[协议](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/protocol-zh)。

> [域名系统区(DNS 区)](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zone-zh)

> [DNS](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zh) [域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)空间中获得管理权[授权](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/delegation-zh)的部分。例如，如果将[根区](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/root-zone-zh)的不同部分[授权](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/delegation-zh)为[顶级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh) ([TLD](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh))，则每个 [TLD](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh) 都将成为一个具有独立管理权的 [DNS 区](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zone-zh)。同样地，如果某个 [TLD](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh) 将其[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)空间划分为多个[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh)，则一般会授予对每个[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh)的管理权限，从而创建更多 [DNS 区](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zone-zh)。

> 处于 [DNS](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zh) 层级结构中任意级别的[父域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/parent-domain-zh)名可以选择性地授予对其任意或所有[子域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-zh)的管理权。“区”始终从某个[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)边界开始，它包含一个旨在标识主机服务器具有管理权的[域文件](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/zone-file-zh)，然后会在另一个具有独立管理权的区的边界结束。

> [顶级域(TLD)](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh)

> 在[域名系统](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zh) ([DNS](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zh)) 的[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)层次中处于最顶层的域。在[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)中，[TLD](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh) 显示在[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh)之后。例如，在[域名](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh) **icann.org** 中，[字符](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/character-zh) **org** 代表 [TLD](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh)。

> [TLD](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh) 的管理者负责控制在该 [TLD](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh) 下可识别哪些[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh)。[TLD](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh) 分为两类：[通用顶级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/generic-top-level-domain-zh)（例如，.com、.net、.edu）和[国家和地区顶级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/country-code-top-level-domain-zh)（例如，.jp、.de、.in）。

> [二级域(SLD)](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh)

> 在[顶级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh)下面的[区域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/zone-zh)中找到的[标签](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-zh)。在“[example.com](http://example.com)”中，[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh)为“example”。

> [子域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-zh)

> 在[域名系统](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zh) ([DNS](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zh)) 层次结构中，位于上一级域之下的域。例如，域 **community.icann.org** 和 **gnso.icann.org** 是[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh) **icann.org** 的[子域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-zh)。域 **icann.org** 是[顶级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-zh) **.org** 的[子域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-zh)。

> [子域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-zh)可以[授权](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/delegation-zh)给特定的实体。例如，[注册服务机构](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/registrar-zh)将[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh)授权给[注册人](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/registrant-zh)。然后，[注册人](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/registrant-zh)可以将其[二级域](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-zh)内的子域[授权](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/delegation-zh)给其他实体。

# EN

> [domain name](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-en)

> A unique name that forms the basis of the [uniform resource locator](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-en)s ([URL](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-en)s) that people use to find resources on the Internet (e.g., web pages, email servers, images, and videos). The [domain name](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-en) itself identifies a specific address on the Internet that belongs to an entity such as a company, organization, institution, or individual. For example, in the [URL](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-en) **Public Comment**, the [domain name](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-en) **icann.org** directs a browser to the [ICANN organization](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/icann-organization-en)’s domain. The rest of the [URL](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-en) directs the browser to a specific resource on the **www** server within [ICANN](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/internet-corporation-for-assigned-names-and-numbers-en)’s domain (in this case, the [Public Comment](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/public-comment-en)s page on the [ICANN](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/internet-corporation-for-assigned-names-and-numbers-en) org website).

> A [domain name](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-en) consists of two or more textual segments separated by dots. For example, in the [domain name](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-en) **icann.org**, the first part of the name, **icann**, represents a [second-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-en) within the [top-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en) **org**. Domain names can also have more than two segments, as in **bbc.co.uk**. In this example, **bbc** represents a [subdomain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-en) within the [second-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-en) **co**, which resides in the [top-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en) **uk**.

> [label](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-en)

> The segments that are separated by dot [character](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/character-en)s in a [domain name](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-en). For example, the name **gnso.icann.org** consists of three [label](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-en)s: **gnso**, **icann**, and **org**. Each [label](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-en) in the name, when read from first to last, represents a [subdomain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-en) of the [label](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-en) that follows it. For example, the [label](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-en)s in the name **gnso.icann.org** indicate that **gnso** is a [subdomain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-en) of the **icann** domain, and **icann** is a [subdomain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-en) of the **org** domain.

> In the early days of the [Domain Name System](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-en) ([DNS](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-en)), [label](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-en)s could be composed only of [character](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/character-en)s from the Latin alphabet (a to z), European-Arabic digits (0 to 9), and the hyphen (-). The [DNS](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-en) now supports *Internationalized Domain Names*, which allow [label](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-en)s to include [character](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/character-en)s from other alphabets (e.g., Arabic and Cyrillic) and languages (e.g., Chinese, Japanese, Korean).

> [uniform resource locator(URL)](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-en)

> A globally unique sequence of [character](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/character-en)s that describes the location of a specific file or resource (e.g., streaming video, software application, online service) on the Internet. The [URL](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-en) also identifies the [protocol](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/protocol-en) to use to open the specified file or resource. In the [URL](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/uniform-resource-locator-en), [ICANN Lookup](https://whois.icann.org/en), **https** identifies the [protocol](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/protocol-en) to use to open the resource located at **ICANN Lookup**.

> [Domain Name System zone(DNS zone)](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zone-en) a.k.a zone

> A segment of the [DNS](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-en) namespace to which administrative authority has been delegated. For example, when sections of the [root zone](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/root-zone-en) are delegated as [top-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en)s ([TLD](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en)s), each [TLD](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en) becomes an independently administered [DNS zone](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zone-en). Likewise, when a [TLD](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en) divides its namespace into [second-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-en)s, it generally delegates administrative authority to each of those domains, thus creating additional [DNS zone](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-zone-en)s.

> A [parent domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/parent-domain-en) at any level in the [DNS](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-en) hierarchy can optionally delegate administrative authority to any or all its [subdomain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-en)s (children). A [zone](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/zone-en) always starts at a domain boundary and includes a [zone file](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/zone-file-en) identifies the host servers over which it has administrative authority. A [zone](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/zone-en) ends at the boundary of another independently administered [zone](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/zone-en).

> [top-level domain(TLD)](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en)

> A domain at the top of the naming hierarchy of the [Domain Name System](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-en). In a [domain name](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-en), the [TLD](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en) appears after the [second-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-en). For example, in the [domain name](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-en) **icann.org**, the [character](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/character-en)s **org** identify the [TLD](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en).

> The administrators of a [TLD](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en) control which [second-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-en)s are recognized within the [TLD](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en). [TLD](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en)s fall into two classes: [generic top-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/generic-top-level-domain-en)s (e.g., .com, .net, .edu) and [country code top-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/country-code-top-level-domain-en)s (e.g., .jp, .de, .in).

> [second-level domain(SLD)](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-en)

> A [label](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/label-en) that is found in a [zone](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/zone-en) that is under a [top-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en). In *example.com*, the [second-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-en) is *example*.

> [subdomain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-en)

> A domain that resides within a higher-level domain in the [Domain Name System](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-system-en) hierarchy. For example, the domains **community.icann.org** and **gnso.icann.org** are [subdomain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-en)s of the [second-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-en) **icann.org**. The domain **icann.org** is a [subdomain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-en) of the [top-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/top-level-domain-en) **.org**.

> Subdomains can be delegated to specific entities. Registrars, for example, delegate [second-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-en)s to [registrant](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/registrant-en)s. The [registrant](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/registrant-en)s can then delegate [subdomain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/subdomain-en)s within their [second-level domain](https://www.icann.org/en/icann-acronyms-and-terms/second-level-domain-en)s.

# 补充

### 域名 vs  URL（统一资源定位符）

- 域名是互联网中的一个易于记忆的名称，用来标识一个特定的IP地址。

- URL是一个完整的地址，用来标识和定位互联网上的特定资源。URL不仅包括域名，还包含协议、路径、端口、查询参数等，用来精确定位和访问资源。

> 拓展：URI（统一资源标识符，Uniform Resource Identifier）和 URL（统一资源定位符，Uniform Resource Locator）是相关但不完全相同的概念。

> 区别：

>

> **定义**：

>

>

> **URI**：是用来唯一标识互联网上的资源的字符串序列，可以标识任何资源，包括但不限于 Web 页面、文件、服务、电子邮件地址等。

> **URL**：是 URI 的一种，它除了唯一标识资源外，还提供了如何访问这些资源的具体信息，包括协议（如`http`、`https`）、主机名、路径等。

>

>

> **功能**：

>

>

> **URI**：用于唯一标识和命名资源，不一定包含访问资源的具体方式和位置信息。

> **URL**：除了唯一标识资源外，还包括了如何访问资源的详细信息，更为具体。

>

> 联系：

>

> **URL 是 URI 的子集**：

>

>

> 每个 URL 都是一个 URI，因为 URL 提供了唯一标识和定位互联网上的资源的信息。URI 是一个更广泛的概念，它包括了 URL 和 URN（统一资源名称）两种形式。

>

>

> **共同点**：

>

>

> 都是用来唯一标识互联网上的资源。

> 都遵循 URI 的统一格式标准。

>

> 举例：

>

> **URI**：`mailto:user@example.com`

>

> 这是一个 URI，标识了一个电子邮件地址，**但它不是一个 URL**，因为它没有提供访问资源的具体路径和协议信息。

>

>

> **URL**：`https://www.example.com/index.html`

>

> 这是一个 URL（**同时也是一个 URI**）。它不仅唯一标识了资源（`index.html`文件），还提供了访问该资源所需的协议（`https`）、主机名（`www.example.com`）、路径（`/index.html`）等信息。

### .com .xyz一类“后缀”算不算域名

个人认为**算域(domain)，不算域名（domain name)**

- 从定义来看，一个[域名（domain name）](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/domain-name-zh)由两个或多个[字符串](https://www.icann.org/zh/icann-acronyms-and-terms/string-zh)构成，中间用句点分隔开来。那么纯TLD不能称之为域名。

- 从命名上看，TLD的D就是域（domain)的意思
