# Trees Are Everywhere

+   - The tree structure of evolution is even more clearly shown in Ernst Haeckel’s diagram of the tree of life from 1866.
+   - The details of evolutionary tree diagrams have changed markedly over the last 150 years, but their general structure—starting with a single root and branching to form new lineages—remains constant.
