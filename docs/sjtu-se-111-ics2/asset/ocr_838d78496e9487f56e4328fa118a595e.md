# Pricing & Distribution Strategy

Our pricing model is designed to maximize adoption while ensuring sustainable revenue growth. We employ a tiered subscription approach with a free tier for individual users and premium enterprise plans.

The distribution strategy focuses on digital channels, strategic partnerships, and community-led growth initiatives to expand market reach efficiently.

Revenue projections show a steady increase over the next three quarters, driven by new customer acquisition and expansion within existing accounts.
