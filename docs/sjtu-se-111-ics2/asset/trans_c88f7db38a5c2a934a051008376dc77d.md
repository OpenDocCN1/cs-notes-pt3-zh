  - 组合逻辑检测停顿条件
  - 设置模式信号以决定流水线寄存器如何更新

# 流水线控制的初始版本

```verilog
bool F_stall =
    d_srcA == E_dstE || d_srcA == E_dstM ||
    d_srcA == M_dstE || d_srcA == M_dstM ||
    d_srcA == W_dstE || d_srcA == W_dstM;

bool D_stall =
    d_srcA == E_dstE || d_srcA == E_dstM ||
    d_srcA == M_dstE || d_srcA == M_dstM ||
    d_srcA == W_dstE || d_srcA == W_dstM;

bool E_bubble =
    d_srcA == E_dstE || d_srcA == E_dstM ||
    d_srcA == M_dstE || d_srcA == M_dstM ||
    d_srcA == W_dstE || d_srcA == W_dstM;
```
