# Packages versus Subsystems

# Subsystems

+   - Provide behavior
+   - Completely encapsulate their contents
+   - Are easily replaced

# Packages

+   - Don’t provide behavior
+   - Don’t completely encapsulate their contents
+   - May not be easily replaced
