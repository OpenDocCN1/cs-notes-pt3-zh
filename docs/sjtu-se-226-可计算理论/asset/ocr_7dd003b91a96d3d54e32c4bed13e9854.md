# Definition by Cases

Suppose $f_1(\vec{x}),...,f_k(\vec{x})$ are recursive and $M_1(\vec{x}),...,M_k(\vec{x})$ are partially decidable. For every $\vec{x}$ at most one of $M_1(\vec{x}),...,M_k(\vec{x})$ holds. Then the function $g(\vec{x})$ given by

$g(\vec{x}) \simeq \begin{cases} f_1(\vec{x}), & \text{if } M_1(\vec{x}) \text{ holds}, \\ f_2(\vec{x}), & \text{if } M_2(\vec{x}) \text{ holds}, \\ \vdots \\ f_k(\vec{x}), & \text{if } M_k(\vec{x}) \text{ holds}. \end{cases}$

is recursive.
