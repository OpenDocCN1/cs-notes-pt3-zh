$G_{u,v} = \frac{1}{4}\alpha(u)\alpha(v)\sum_{x=0}^{7}\sum_{y=0}^{7} g_{x,y} \cos\left[\frac{(2x+1)u\pi}{16}\right] \cos\left[\frac{(2y+1)v\pi}{16}\right]$\n\nwhere\n\n• $u$ is the horizontal spatial frequency, for the integers $0 \le u < 8$.\n• $v$ is the vertical spatial frequency, for the integers $0 \le v < 8$\n• $\alpha(u) = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}, & \text{if } u = 0 \\ 1, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$ is a normalizing scale factor to make the transformation orthonormal\n• $g_{x,y}$ is the pixel value at coordinates $(x,y)$\n• $G_{u,v}$ is the DCT coefficient at coordinates $(u,v).$
