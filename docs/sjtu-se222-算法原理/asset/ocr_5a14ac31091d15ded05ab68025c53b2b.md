# Binary Heap

A *max-heap* (*min-heap*) is an *almost complete* *binary tree* with vertices satisfying the *max-heap* (*min-heap*) *property*:

*If v and p(v) are a vertex and its parent respectively, then p(v) ≥ v (p(v) ≤ v).*
