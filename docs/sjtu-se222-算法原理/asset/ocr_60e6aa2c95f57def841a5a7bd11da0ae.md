# Subset sum

Problem: SubsetSum
Input: A set of numbers A={a₁,a₂,...,aₙ}, and a sum s
Output: Yes, if there exists a subset B ⊆ A such that the sum of B equals to s; No, otherwise.

Problem: SubsetSumOpt
Input: A set of numbers A={a₁,a₂,...,aₙ}, and a sum s
Output: a subset of the items that maximizes the total sum of their sizes without exceeding the sum s.
