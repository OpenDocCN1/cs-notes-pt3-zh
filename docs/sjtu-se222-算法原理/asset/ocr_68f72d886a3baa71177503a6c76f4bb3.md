An edge (u,v) is a **back edge** iff

pre(u) > pre(v) && post(u) < post(v)

| type | edge(u,v) |
|---|---|
| tree | [u [v ] v ] u |
| back | [v [u ] u ] v |
| forward | [u [v ] v ] u |
| cross | [v ] v [u ] u |
