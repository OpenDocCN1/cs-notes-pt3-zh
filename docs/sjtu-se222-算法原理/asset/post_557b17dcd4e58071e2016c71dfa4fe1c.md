## Flow Networks

![](img/a8f1b433a70f1e5c410c8b43e41b650e_838_0.png)

A *flow network* is

- 1. a directed graph G<V,E>,
- 2. Weights on edges denote the *capacities*.
- 3. Two distinguished vertex: s and t, denoting *source* and *sink*.

Definition: *st-Flow* A function f: V×V→R is a *st-flow (flow)* if satisfying:

∀ u,v∈V. f(u,v) = -f(v,u).
∀ u,v∈V. f(u,v) ≤ c(u,v).
∀ u ∈ V\{s,t}. Σv∈V f(u,v) = 0.
∀ v∈V. f(v,v)=0.

The *value* of a flow f:

|f| = f(s,V) = Σv∈V f(s,v)
|f| ≥ 0

## Minimum Cut

Definition: Cut
A cut {S,T} is a partition of the vertex set V into two subsets S and T such that s ∈ S and t ∈ T.
The capacity of the cut {S,T}, denoted by c(S,T), is
c(S,T) = Σ_{u∈S, v∈T} c(u,v)

![](img/a8f1b433a70f1e5c410c8b43e41b650e_840_0.png)

Lemma
For any cut {S,T} and a flow f, |f| = f(S,T) ≤ c(S,T).
