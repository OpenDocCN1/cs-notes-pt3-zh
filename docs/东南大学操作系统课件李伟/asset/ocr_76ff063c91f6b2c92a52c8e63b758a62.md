# Example of Multilevel Feedback Queue

+   - Three queues:
+     - Q₀ – time quantum 8 milliseconds
+     - Q₁ – time quantum 16 milliseconds
+     - Q₂ – FCFS
+   - Scheduling
+     - A new job enters Q₀ which is served RR. When it gains CPU, job runs for 8 milliseconds. If it does not finish in 8 milliseconds, job is moved to Q₁.
+     - At Q₁ job is again served RR and runs for 16 additional milliseconds. If it still does not complete, it is preempted and moved to queue Q₂.
