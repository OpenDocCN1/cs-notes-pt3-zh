From (10.2), if c_ij is to be minimal, then cost(L)=c_{i,k-1} and cost(R)=c_{kj}, as otherwise we could replace either L or R by a subtree with a lower cost, thus generating a b.s.t. for a_{i+1},......, a_j with a lower cost than c_ij. This violates the assumption that T_ij is optimal.

Hence, E.q.(10.2) becomes

c_ij = p_k + w_{i,k-1} + w_{kj} + c_{i,k-1} + c_{kj}
= w_{ij} + c_{i,k-1} + c_{kj}      (10.3)
