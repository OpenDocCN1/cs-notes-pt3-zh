# Analysis of TopologicalOrder:

Very efficient because of a judicious choice of data structure.

+   - Lines 4-5 loop: O(n)

+   - Lines 6-10 totally O(n)

+   - The while loop of 11-15 takes O(dᵢ) for each vertex i, where dᵢ is the out-degree of i. The total time for this part is O((∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿ⁻¹ dᵢ)+n)=O(e+n).

The total time: O(e+n).
