(3) Having selected a vertex u as in (2), u becomes a member of S. Now dist[w], w ∉S, may change. If it does, it must be due to a shorter path from v to u and then to w.

![](bbox=[0.07, 0.3, 0.85, 0.7])

The v to u path must be the shortest v to u path, and the u to w path is just the edge <u, w>. So if dist[w] is to decrease, it is because the current dist[w]>dist[u]+length(u, w).
