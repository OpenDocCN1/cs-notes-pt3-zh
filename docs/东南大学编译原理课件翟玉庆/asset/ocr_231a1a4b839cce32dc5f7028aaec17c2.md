# 2)Strategy for elimination of Left Recursion

Convert left recursion into the equivalent
right recursion

$P \to P\alpha \mid \beta$

$\Rightarrow P \to \beta\alpha^*$

$\Rightarrow P \to \beta P'$,  $P' \to \alpha P' \mid \varepsilon$
