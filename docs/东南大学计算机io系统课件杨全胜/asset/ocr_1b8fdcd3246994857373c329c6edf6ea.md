# 4.2 8259A PIC

# Operation and commands

# Initialization Command Words 3(ICW3)

| A0 | D7 | D6 | D5 | D4 | D3 | D2 | D1 | D0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | S7 | S6 | S5 | S4 | S3 | S2 | S1 | S0 |
ICW3 of master controller

| A0 | D7 | D6 | D5 | D4 | D3 | D2 | D1 | D0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | × | × | × | × | × | ID2 | ID1 | ID0 |
ICW3 of slave controller

For the master controller, ICW3 is used to indicate which IRQ input line is used to cascade the slave controller. The corresponding bit in ICW3 is set to a 1.

For the slave controller, ICW3 is the slave identification code used during an interrupt acknowledge cycle.
