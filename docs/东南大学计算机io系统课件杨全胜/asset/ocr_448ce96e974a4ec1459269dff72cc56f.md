# Frequency Divider Design

Using 8254(address:40H~43H), Convert 5MHz pulse to 1Hz pulse

Initial count =f_CLK/f_OUT=5×10⁶>65536, How to do it?

![](bbox=[0.06, 0.54, 0.38, 0.89])

```
MOV AL, 00110111B; T/C₀
OUT 43H, AL
MOV AX, 5000H
OUT 40H, AL
MOV AL, AH
OUT 40H, AL
MOV AL, 01110101B; T/C₁
OUT 43H, AL
MOV AX, 1000H
OUT 41H, AL
MOV AL, AH
OUT 41H, AL
```
