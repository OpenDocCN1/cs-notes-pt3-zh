# Operation and commands

# Operation Control Word 1 (OCW1)

| A0 | D7 | D6 | D5 | D4 | D3 | D2 | D1 | D0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | M7 | M6 | M5 | M4 | M3 | M2 | M1 | M0 |

1=masked 0=enable

OCW1 sets and clears the mask bits in the Interrupt Mask Register (IMR).

M7~M0 represent the eight mask bits.

+   - M = 1 indicates the channel is masked (inhibited),
+   - M = 0 indicates the channel is enabled.
