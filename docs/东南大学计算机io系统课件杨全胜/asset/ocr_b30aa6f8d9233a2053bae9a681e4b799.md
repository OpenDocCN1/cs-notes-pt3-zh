# Square Wave Generator with two frequency

![](bbox=[0.07, 0.24, 0.72, 0.66])

```
MOV AL, 00110111B; T/C0
OUT 43H, AL
MOV AX, 1000H
OUT 40H, AL
MOV AL, AH
OUT 40H, AL
```

```
MOV AL, 01110111B; T/C1
OUT 43H, AL
MOV AX, 1000H
OUT 41H, AL
MOV AL, AH
OUT 41H, AL
MOV AL, 10010111B; T/C2
OUT 43H, AL
MOV AX, 5H
OUT 42H, AL
```
