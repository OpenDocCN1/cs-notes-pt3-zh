# 4.3 Interrupt in x86 System

+   - 8259A in PCH
+     - Set the interrupt request mode in Intel chipset
+       - In the PCH, bit3 in ICW1 is disabled and a new register for edge and level triggered mode selection.

ELCR1——For master controller (address 4D0H)

| 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IRQ7 | IRQ6 | IRQ5 | IRQ4 | IRQ3 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

ELCR2——For slave controller (address 4D1H)

| 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IRQ15 | IRQ14 | 0 | IRQ12 | IRQ11 | IRQ10 | IRQ9 | 0 |

IRQx=0 : IRQx is edge trigger mode

IRQx=1 : IRQx is level trigger mode
