CR_M and CR_L are two 8-bit registers. Both are normally referred to as one unit and called just CR (Count Register).

The actual counter is labeled CE (Counting Element). It is a 16-bit presettable synchronous down

OL_M and OL_L are two 8-bit latches. Both are normally referred to as one unit called just OL (" Output Latch").
