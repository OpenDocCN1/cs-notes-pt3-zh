+   - a constant weight \(w(v)>0\) (for the analysis, this can be arbitrary)
+   - \(weight\ sum\ s(x)=\sum_{y\in subtree(x)}w(y)\) (where subtree(x) is the subtree rooted at \(x\), including \(x\))
+   - \(rank\ r(x)=\log s(x)\)

We use \(r(x)\) as the potential of a node. The potential function after \(i\) operations is thus \(\Phi_i=\sum_{x\in tree(T)}r(x)\).
