- a constant weight \(w(v)>0\) (for the analysis, this can be arbitrary)
- \(weight\ sum\ s(x)=\sum_{y\in subtree(x)}w(y)\) (where subtree(x) is the subtree rooted at \(x\), including \(x\))
- \(rank\ r(x)=\log s(x)\)

We use \(r(x)\) as the potential of a node. The potential function after \(i\) operations is thus \(\Phi_i=\sum_{x\in tree(T)}r(x)\).

## Lemma 1 The amortized cost of a splay step on node x is ≤ 3(r′(x) − r(x)) + 1, where r is rank before the splay step and r′ is rank after the splay step. Furthermore, the amortized cost of the rr, ll, lr, and rl splay steps is ≤ 3(r′(x) − r(x)).

![](img/e8cfdb0a049dc5ab9d802234ebc2190e_82_0.png)

We will consider only the rr splay step (refer to Figure 3.1). The actual cost of the splay step is 2 (for 2 rotations). The splay step only affects the potentials/ranks of nodes x, y, and z; we observe that r′(x) = r(z), r(y) ≥ r(x), and r′(y) ≤ r′(x).

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{amortized cost} &= 2+\phi(i+1)-\phi(i)\\
&=2+\bigl(r'(x)+r'(y)+r'(z)\bigr)-\bigl(r(x)+r(y)-r(z)\bigr)\\
&=2+\bigl(r'(x)-r(z)\bigr)+r'(y)+r'(z)-r(x)-r(y)\\
&\le 2+0+r'(x)+r'(z)-r(x)-r(x)\\
&=2+r'(x)+r'(z)-2r'(x)
\end{aligned}
\]

The log function is concave, i.e., \(\frac{\log a+\log b}{2}\le \log\left(\frac{a+b}{2}\right)\). Thus we also have (\(s\) is weight sum before the splay step and \(s'\) is weight sum after the splay step):

\[
\frac{\log(s(x))+\log(s'(z))}{2}\le \log\left(\frac{s(x)+s'(z)}{2}\right)
\]

\[
\frac{r(x)+r'(z)}{2}\le \log\left(\frac{s(x)+s'(z)}{2}\right)\qquad\text{(note that }s(x)+s'(z)\le s'(x)\text{)}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\le \log\left(\frac{s'(x)}{2}\right)\\
&=r'(x)-1\\
r'(z)&\le 2r'(x)-r(x)-2
\end{aligned}
\]

## **Corollary 1**  *The amortized cost of a splay operation on node x is O(log n).*

## **Proof:**

The amortized cost of a splay operation on *x* is the sum of the amortized costs of the splay steps on *x* involved:

```
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{amortized cost} &= \sum_i \text{cost}(\text{splay step}_i) \\
&\leq \sum_i \left(3(r^{i+1}(x)-r^i(x))+1\right) \\
&= 3(r(\text{root})-r(x))+1
\end{aligned}$$
```

The +1 comes from the last *r* or *r* splay step (if necessary). If we set *w(v) = 1* for all nodes in the tree, then *r(root) = log n* and we have:

```
$$\text{amortized cost} \leq 3\log n + 1 = O(\log n)$$
```
