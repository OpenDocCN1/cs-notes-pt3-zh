# 支持向量机

+   🔴 **处理噪声和离群点**

引入松弛变量 $\xi_n \geq 0$

$$\begin{aligned}\xi_n &= 0\\\xi_n &= |t_n-y(x_n)|\end{aligned}$$

$$\xi_n=0$$
$$0<\xi_n\leq 1$$
$$\xi_n>1$$

**原有约束** $t_n y(x_n)\geq 1$ ➡️ $t_n y(x_n)\geq 1-\xi_n$
