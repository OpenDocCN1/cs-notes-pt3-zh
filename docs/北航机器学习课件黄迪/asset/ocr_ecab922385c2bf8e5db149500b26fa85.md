$\nabla J_P(a) = \frac{\partial J_P(a)}{\partial a} = \sum_{y \in \eta^k} (-y)$

$a(k+1) = a(k) - \rho_k \nabla J$

$a(k+1) = a(k) + \rho_k \nabla \sum_{y \in \eta^k} y$
