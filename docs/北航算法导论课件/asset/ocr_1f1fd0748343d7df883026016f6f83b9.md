# 16.2.3 Greedy vs. dynamic programming

+   - **0-1 knapsack problem**  
+     has not the greedy-choice property
+   - **W = 50.**
+   - **Greedy solution:**
+     - take items 1 and 2
+     - value = 160, weight = 30
+   - **20 pounds of capacity leftover.**

| j | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| vᵢ | 60 | 100 | 120 |
| wᵢ | 10 | 20 | 30 |
| vᵢ / wᵢ | 6 | 5 | 4 |

+   - **Optimal solution:**
+     - Take items 2 and 3
+     - value=220, weight=50
+   - **No leftover capacity.** (没有剩余空间)
