```
MERGE(A, p, q, r)
1   n₁ ← q-p+1
2   n₂ ← r-q
3   create arrays L[1 .. n₁+1] and R[1 .. n₂+1]
4   for i←1 to n₁
5       do L[i]←A[p+i-1]
6   for j←1 to n₂
7       do R[j]←A[q+j]
8   L[n₁+1]←∞    // To avoid having to check whether either pile is empty in each
9   R[n₂+1]←∞    // basic step, a sentinel card is put on the bottom of each pile.
10  i←1
11  j←1
12  for k←p to r
13      do if L[i]≤R[j]
14          then A[k]←L[i]
15              i←i+1
16          else A[k]←R[j]
17              j←j+1
```
