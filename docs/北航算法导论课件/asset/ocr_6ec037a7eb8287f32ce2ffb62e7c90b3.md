# 30.2.4 Interpolation at the complex roots of unity

Lemma 30.6 (Summation lemma, 求和引理)
For any integer n≥1 and nonnegative integer k not divisible by n , (k ≠ m·n), we have
∑_{j=0}^{n-1} (ω_n^k)^j = 0.

Theorem 30.7
For j, k = 0, 1, …, n-1, the (j, k) entry of V_n^{-1} is ω_n^{-kj} / n.
