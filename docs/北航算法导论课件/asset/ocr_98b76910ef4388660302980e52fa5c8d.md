+   - **Matrix-chain multiplication**
+     - constrain subproblem space to matrix products $A_1A_2\ldots A_j$,
+     - an optimal parenthesization split this product between $A_k$ and $A_{k+1}$ for some $1\le k<j$,
+     - we had subproblems of the form $A_1A_2\ldots A_k$ and $A_{k+1}A_{k+2}\ldots A_j$,
+     - the latter subproblem is not of the form $A_1A_2\ldots A_j$,
+     - it was necessary to allow our subproblems to vary at “both ends”, that is, to allow both $i$ and $j$ to vary in the subproblem $A_iA_{i+1}\ldots A_j$.
