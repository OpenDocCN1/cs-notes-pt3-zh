# Step 4: Constructing the fastest way through the factory

+   - Use \(f[i], f^*, l[i],\) and \(l^*\), to construct the sequence of stations used in the fastest way.
+   
+   （使用 \(f\) 和 \(l\) 去构造最快路径）
+   
+   - PRINT-STATION procedure prints out the stations used, in decreasing order of station #.

| j | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |

| l₂[j] | 1 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 |

\(l^* = 1\)

```
PRINT-STATIONS(l, n)
1  i ← l*
2  print "station " n ", line " i
3  for j ← n downto 2
4      do i ← lᵢ[j]
5         print "station " j-1 ", line " i
```
