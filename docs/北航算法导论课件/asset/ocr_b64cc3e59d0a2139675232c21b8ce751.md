Multiplying polynomial C(x)=A(x)B(x)

Can we use the linear-time multiplication method for polynomials in point-value form to expedite(加速) polynomial multiplication in coefficient form? 〔对于多项式乘法，能否依据点值形式的线性乘法来改进系数形式的乘法？〕

The answer hinges on our ability to convert a polynomial quickly from coefficient form to point-value and vice-versa. 〔该问题依赖于我们将多项式从系数表示快速转换为点值表示的能力，或相反。〕
