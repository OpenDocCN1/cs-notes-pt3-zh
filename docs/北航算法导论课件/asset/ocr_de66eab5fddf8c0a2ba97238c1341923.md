# 30.2.4 Interpolation at the complex roots of unity

DFT

\[
y_k=A(\omega_n^k)=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}a_j\omega_n^{kj},\quad (k=0,1,\cdots,n-1) \tag{30.8}
\]

\[
y=A(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}a_jx^j
\]

inverse DFT

\[
a_j=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}y_k\omega_n^{-kj},\quad (j=0,1,\cdots,n-1) \tag{30.11}
\]

+   • **The inverse DFT（逆DFT） can be computed in Θ(n lg n) time, for (30.8), by replacing \(\omega_n\) by \(\omega_n^{-1}\), and divide each element of the result by \(n\).**
