# 30.2.2 The DFT

+   - wish to evaluate a polynomial $A(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a_j x^j$ of degree-bound $n$ at $x = \omega_n^0, \omega_n^1, \omega_n^2, \cdots, \omega_n^{n-1}$.
+   - without loss of generality, assume that $n=2^m$, if not, let $a_{n+k}=0$
+   - Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT, 离散傅里叶变换)
+     let $A$ is given in coefficient form: $a=(a_0, a_1, ..., a_{n-1})^T$, let $x_k = \omega_n^k$, define $y_k$, for $k = 0, 1, ..., n-1$, by
+     $y_k = A(\omega_n^k) = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a_j \omega_n^{kj}$, $
+     \begin{pmatrix} y_0 \\ y_1 \\ \vdots \\ y_{n-1} \end{pmatrix} = 
+     \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\ 1 & \omega_n^1 & \omega_n^2 & \cdots & \omega_n^{n-1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 1 & \omega_n^{(n-1)1} & \omega_n^{(n-1)2} & \cdots & \omega_n^{(n-1)(n-1)} \end{pmatrix} 
+     \begin{pmatrix} a_0 \\ a_1 \\ \vdots \\ a_{n-1} \end{pmatrix}$
+     also write $y = \text{DFT}_n(a)$.
+     Take time $\Theta(n^2)$ to compute straightforward? (不好！)
