
## 30.2.1 Complex roots of unity
### some properties
1) \(\omega_n^n=\omega_n^0=1\)

\(\left(\omega_n=e^{2\pi i/n}=\cos 2\pi+i\sin 2\pi\right)\)

2) \(\omega_n^j\omega_n^k=\omega_n^{j+k}=\omega_n^{(j+k)\bmod n}\)

\(\text{(if }j+k=mn+r,\text{ then}\)

\(\omega_n^{j+k}=\omega_n^{mn+r}=\omega_n^{mn}\omega_n^r=(\omega_n^n)^m\omega_n^r=\omega_n^r=\omega_n^{(j+k)\bmod n}\text{)}\)

3) \(\omega_n^{-1}=\omega_n^{n-1}\)

(proof by using \(\omega_n^k=e^{2\pi ik/n},\ k=0,1,\cdots,n-1\))

### Lemma 30.3 (Cancellation lemma, 可约（相消）引理)
For any integers n≥0, k≥0, and d >0,

ω_{dn}^{dk} = ω_n^{k}.  (30.7)

Proof  ω_{dn}^{dk} = (e^{2πi / dn})^{dk} = (e^{2πi / n})^{k} = ω_n^{k}.

### Lemma 30.5 (Halving lemma, 等分引理)

If n > 0 is even, then the squares of the n complex nth roots of unity are the n/2 complex (n/2)th roots of unity.

〔n 个单位 n 次复方根的平方是 n/2 个 n/2 次复方根〕

(ωₙᵏ)² = ω₂₍ₙ/₂₎²ᵏ = ωₙ/₂ᵏ , k = 0,1,…, n/2 −1

## 30.2.1 Complex roots of unity
### Lemma 30.6 (Summation lemma, 求和引理)

For any integer $n\ge1$ and nonnegative integer $k$ not divisible by $n$, $(k \ne m\cdot n)$, we have

$$\sum_{j=0}^{n-1} (\omega_n^k)^j = 0.$$

### Proof

$$\sum_{j=0}^{n-1} (\omega_n^k)^j = \frac{(\omega_n^k)^n - 1}{\omega_n^k - 1} = \frac{(\omega_n^n)^k - 1}{\omega_n^k - 1} = \frac{(1)^k - 1}{\omega_n^k - 1} = 0,\ k \ne mn.$$

（证明思想：利用实数的等幂级数求和性质）

## 30.2.2 The DFT
- wish to evaluate a polynomial $A(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a_j x^j$ of degree-bound $n$ at $x = \omega_n^0, \omega_n^1, \omega_n^2, \cdots, \omega_n^{n-1}$.
- without loss of generality, assume that $n=2^m$, if not, let $a_{n+k}=0$
- Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT, 离散傅里叶变换)
  let $A$ is given in coefficient form: $a=(a_0, a_1, ..., a_{n-1})^T$, let $x_k = \omega_n^k$, define $y_k$, for $k = 0, 1, ..., n-1$, by
- $y_k = A(\omega_n^k) = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a_j \omega_n^{kj}$, $
- $\begin{pmatrix} y_0 \\ y_1 \\ \vdots \\ y_{n-1} \end{pmatrix} = 
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\ 1 & \omega_n^1 & \omega_n^2 & \cdots & \omega_n^{n-1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 1 & \omega_n^{(n-1)1} & \omega_n^{(n-1)2} & \cdots & \omega_n^{(n-1)(n-1)} \end{pmatrix} 
- $\begin{pmatrix} a_0 \\ a_1 \\ \vdots \\ a_{n-1} \end{pmatrix}$
- also write $y = \text{DFT}_n(a)$.
- Take time $\Theta(n^2)$ to compute straightforward? (不好！)

## 30.2.3 The FFT
A(x) = a₀ + a₁x + a₂x² + a₃x³ + ⋯ + aₙ₋₁xⁿ⁻¹

- **Fast Fourier Transform (FFT, 快速傅里叶变换)**
- takes advantage of the special properties of the complex roots of unity
- we can compute DFTₙ(a) in time Θ(n log n)
- employs a divide-and-conquer strategy,
  even-index, A^[0](x) = a₀ + a₂x + a₄x² + ... + aₙ₋₂xⁿ/²⁻¹
  odd-index, A^[1](x) = a₁ + a₃x + a₅x² + ... + aₙ₋₁xⁿ/²⁻¹.
- A[0] contains all the even-index coefficients of A (the binary representation of the index ends in 0)
- A[1] contains all the odd-index coefficients (the binary representation of the index ends in 1). It follows that

A(x) = A^[0](x²) + x A^[1](x²)    (30.9)

## 30.2.3 The FFT
- Fast Fourier Transform (FFT, 快速傅里叶变换)
- employs a divide-and-conquer strategy,
  even-index, $A^{[0]}(x) = a_0 + a_2 x + a_4 x^2 + ... + a_{n-2} x^{n/2-1}$
  odd-index, $A^{[1]}(x) = a_1 + a_3 x + a_5 x^2 + ... + a_{n-1} x^{n/2-1}$.
  $A(x) = A^{[0]}(x^2) + x A^{[1]}(x^2)$  (30.9)
  $A(\omega_n^k) = A^{[0]}((\omega_n^k)^2) + \omega_n^k A^{[1]}((\omega_n^k)^2)$

$A(x) = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a_j x^j$

- the problem of evaluating $A(x)$ at $\omega_n^0, \omega_n^1, \omega_n^2, ..., \omega_n^{n-1}$ reduces to
  1) evaluating the $A^{[0]}(x)$ and $A^{[1]}(x)$ at the points
     $(\omega_n^0)^2, (\omega_n^1)^2, ..., (\omega_n^{n/2-1})^2$
     $(\omega_n^{0+n/2})^2, (\omega_n^{1+n/2})^2, ..., (\omega_n^{n/2-1+n/2})^2$
     and then $\omega_{n/2}^0, \omega_{n/2}^1, ..., \omega_{n/2}^{n/2-1}$  (32.10)
  2) combining the results according to equation (30.9).
