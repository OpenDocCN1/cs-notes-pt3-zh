
## Counting the number of parenthesizations

A₁ A₂ A₃ A₄ A₅ : (A₁ A₂ A₃)(A₄ A₅)?   (A₁ A₂)(A₃ A₄ A₅)? ……  
A₁(A₂ A₃)?   (A₁ A₂)A₃?

- Brute force, exhaustively checking all possible parenthesizations.
- P(n): the # of alternative parenthesizations of n matrices.
- （令P(n)表示 n 个矩阵连乘时所有可能的全括号方式的个数）
- n=1, one matrix, one way to parenthesize the matrix product.
- n≥2, product of two fully parenthesized matrix subproducts, and the split between the two subproducts may occur between the kth and (k+1)st matrices for any k = 1, 2, ..., n-1. Thus,
- （n≥2, 矩阵连乘全括号的积是两个‘全括号子矩阵之积’的积。两个全括号子矩阵的分隔位置在A_k和A_{k+1}之间）

P(n) = { 1 , if n = 1,
         Σ_{k=1}^{n-1} P(k)P(n−k), if n ≥ 2. }  (15.11)

- The solution to the recurrence (15.11) is Ω(2ⁿ), a poor strategy.

## Dynamic Programming to solve MCM:

## Four Steps

## Step 1: The structure of an optimal parenthesization

![](img/09c68ab421cfce4995ddad1430ef2637_271_0.png)

- - Find the **optimal substructure**
- - $A_{i..j}$ (where $i \le j$): the product $A_iA_{i+1}\cdots A_kA_{k+1}\cdots A_j$
  - If it is **nontrivial**, i.e. $i<j$, then any parenthesization of the product $A_iA_{i+1}\cdots A_j$ must **split** the product between $A_k$ and $A_{k+1}$ for some integer $k$ in the range $i\le k<j$.
  - （若乘积非平凡，即$i<j$，则任何矩阵连乘全括号必须先全括号子矩阵之积$A_{i..k}$、$A_{k+1..j}$的积，for any $i\le k<j$）
  - First compute $A_{i..k}$, and $A_{k+1..j}$, then $A_{i..k}\cdot A_{k+1..j}=A_{i..j}$

The cost of this parenthesization $A_iA_{i+1}\cdots A_j$

= the cost of computing the matrix $A_{i..k}$ + the cost of computing $A_{k+1..j}$

+ the cost of multiplying $A_{i..k}\cdot A_{k+1..j}$

## Step 1: The structure of an optimal parenthesization

- - **The optimal substructure**
  - The parenthesization of the "prefix" subchain $A_i A_{i+1}...A_k$ within this optimal parenthesization of $A_i A_{i+1}...A_j$ must be an optimal parenthesization of $A_i A_{i+1}...A_k$？（$A_{i,j}$ 的最佳全括号中的 $A_{i..k}$ 的全括号必须是 $A_{i..k}$ 的最佳全括号）

Proof Optimal parenthesization $A_{i,j} = (A_i...A_k)(A_{k+1}...A_j) = M·N$, parenthesization $M = (A_i A_{i+1}...A_k)$ in $A_{i..j}$ above. If there were a less costly way to parenthesize $A_i...A_k = P$, substituting $M$ with $P$, that is $P·N$ would produce another parenthesization of $A_{i..j}$ whose cost was lower than $M·N$. A contradiction.

- - A similar observation holds for $A_{k+1} A_{k+2}...A_j$

## Step 1: The structure of an optimal parenthesization

![](img/09c68ab421cfce4995ddad1430ef2637_274_0.png)

- - Construct an optimal solution to the problem from optimal solutions to subproblems based on optimal substructure.
-   
-     （基于最佳子结构，可以从子问题的最优解构造原问题的最优解）
-   
-   - Any solution to the matrix-chain multiplication requires us to split the product, and **any optimal solution contains within it optimal solutions to subproblem instances.**
-   
-     （任何最优解必包含其子问题的最优解）
-   
-     - **Split** the problem into two subproblems (optimally parenthesizing $A_iA_{i+1}\cdots A_k$ and $A_{k+1}A_{k+2}\cdots A_j$);
-     - **find** optimal solutions to subproblem;
-     - **combine** these optimal subproblem solutions.
-   
-     （将问题分为两个问题，求子问题的最优解，合并子问题的最优解）

## Step 1: The structure of an optimal parenthesization

![](img/09c68ab421cfce4995ddad1430ef2637_275_0.png)

- - When we search for the correct place to split the product, we must consider **all possible places** so that we are sure of having examined the optimal one.
- - （当寻找矩阵连乘分割点时，需要考虑所有分割位置以确保最优解是其中之一）

## Step 2: A recursive solution

A₁ A₂ A₃ A₄ A₅: (A₁ A₂ A₃)(A₄ A₅)?   (A₁ A₂)(A₃ A₄ A₅)? ......  
    A₁ (A₂ A₃)?   (A₁ A₂) A₃?

- Define the cost of an optimal solution recursively in terms of the optimal solutions to subproblems.
- （根据子问题的最优解可以递归地定义原问题的最优解）

- Subproblems: determining the minimum cost of a parenthesization of A_i A_{i+1}...A_j for 1≤i≤j≤n.
- Not A₁ A₂...A_j, Why?
- （子问题：确定矩阵连乘问题的子问题的形式为 A_i A_{i+1}...A_j, for 1≤i≤j≤n）
