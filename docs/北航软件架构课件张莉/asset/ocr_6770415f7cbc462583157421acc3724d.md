# 各Profile中的主要建模概念

| profile核心概念 | SAS | SAD | HPSM | PDL | MAP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 构件 | `<<SASComponent>>` | `<<SADComponent>>` | `<<EJBEnterpriseBean>>`、`<<EJBEntityBean>>`、`<<EJBMessageDrivenBean>>`、`<<EJBSessionBean>>` | `<<PatternTemplate>>`、`<<PatternRole>>`、`<<ComponentRole>>`、`<<ConnectorRole>>`、`<<PortRole>>`、`<<InterfaceRole>>`、`<<AttributeRole>>`、`<<OperationRole>>`、`<<RelationConstraint>>`、`<<PatternInteraction>>` | `<<RefinementMapping>>`，具体映射类型类型包括：`decomposition`、`sharedFunction`、`concretization`、`detailing`、`Orphan` |
| 连接件 | `<<ControlFlow>>`、`<<Data-flow>>`、InformationFlow、Association、Dependency、Usage、`<<call>>`、`<<RPC>>`、`<<MessagePassing>>` | `<<SADConnector>>`、Connector、{assembly, delegation}、`<<RoleBinding>>` | `<<RMI>>`、`<<call>>` | 连接件相关角色及约束 | — |
