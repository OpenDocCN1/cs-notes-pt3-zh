# # Powers of a

Suppose

(S, *) is a semigroup, \( a \in S \quad n \in \mathbb{Z}^+ \)

Define the powers of \( a^n \) recursively as follows:

\( a^1 = a, \quad a^n = a^{n-1} * a, \quad n \geq 2. \)

\( a^0 = e \quad \text{if } (S, *) \text{ is a monoid} \)

if \( m \) and \( n \) are nonnegative integers, then

\( a^m * a^n = a^{m+n} \)
