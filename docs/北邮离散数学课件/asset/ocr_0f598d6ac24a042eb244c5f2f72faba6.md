# Bipartite Graphs

+   - **Def’n.:** A graph $G=(V,E)$ is *bipartite* (two-part) iff $V = V_1 \cap V_2$ where $V_1 \cup V_2=\varnothing$ and $\forall e \in E:$ $\exists v_1 \in V_1, v_2 \in V_2:$ $e=\{v_1,v_2\}$.
+   - **In English:** The graph can be divided into two parts in such a way that all edges go between the two parts.

This definition can easily be adapted for the case of multigraphs and directed graphs as well.

Can represent with zero-one matrices.
