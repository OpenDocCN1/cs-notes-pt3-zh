# # Example 17 - Solution

Step 3: \( f \) is onto.
Suppose that \( b \) is any even integer.
Then \( a = b/2 \in \, \mathbb{Z} \) and
\( f(a) = f(b/2) = 2(b/2) = b \),
so \( f \) is onto.

Step 4: \( f(a+b) = 2(a+b) = 2a+2b = f(a)+f(b) \).
Hence \( (\mathbb{Z}, +) \) and \( (T, +) \) are isomorphic semigroups
