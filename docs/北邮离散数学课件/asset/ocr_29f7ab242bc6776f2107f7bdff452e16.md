# # Theorem 4 - Proof

Let \( t_1 \) and \( t_2 \) are any elements of \( f(S') \),
there exist \( s_1 \) and \( s_2 \) in \( S' \) with \( t_1 = f(s_1) \) and \( t_2 = f(s_2) \).
\( t_1 * t_2 = f(s_1) * f(s_2) = f(s_1 * s_2) = f(s_3) \).
Hence \( t_1 * t_2 \in f(S') \).
Thus \( f(S') \) is closed under the operation \( * \).
Since the associative property holds in \( T \), it holds in \( f(S') \).
So \( f(S') \) is a subsemigroup of \( (T, *) \)
