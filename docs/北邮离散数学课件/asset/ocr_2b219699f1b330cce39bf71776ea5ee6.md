# distinguishable objects and indistinguishable boxes

+   - We can put two employees into one office and two into a second office in exactly three ways, represented by {{A,B}, {C,D}}, {{A,C}, {B,D}}, and {{A,D}, {B,C}}. Finally, we can put two employees into one office, and one each into each of the remaining two offices in six ways, represented by {{A,B}, {C}, {D}}, {{A,C}, {B}, {D}}, {{A,D}, {B}, {C}}, {{B,C}, {A}, {D}}, {{B,D}}, {A}, {C}}, and {{C,D}, {A}, {B}}.
