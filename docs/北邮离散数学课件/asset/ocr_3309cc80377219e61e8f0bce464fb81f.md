# # Syndrome – 特征值

It follows from Corollary I of Section 9.5 that 
\( B^r \) and \( B^n/N \) are isomorphic, where

\( N = \{x \in B^n \mid x^*\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{0}\} = \ker(f_H) = e_H(B^m) \)

under the isomorphism \( g: B^n/N \to B^r \) defined by

\( g(xN) = f_H(x) = x^*\mathbf{H} \)

The element \( x^*\mathbf{H} \) is called the **syndrome** of \( x \)
