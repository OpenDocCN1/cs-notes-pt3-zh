# Theorem 2

Let _R_ be a congruence relation on the semigroup (S, *).

Consider the relation ◦ from S/R × S/R to S/R in which the ordered pair ([_a_], [_b_]) is, for _a_ and _b_ in S, related to [_a_*_b_].

◦([_a_], [_b_]) = [_a_]◦ [_b_] = [_a_*_b_], is a function from S/R×S/R to S/R

(S/R, ◦ ) is a semigroup. S/R is called the **_quotient semigroup(_商半群_)_ or _factor semigroup_**
