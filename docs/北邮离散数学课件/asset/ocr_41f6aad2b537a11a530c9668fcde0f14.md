# Properties of Lattices

+   - Recall the meaning of $a \vee b$ and $a \wedge b$:
+     - $a \le a \vee b$ and $b \le a \vee b$; $a \vee b$ is an *upper bound* of $a$ and $b$.
+     - If $a \le c$ and $b \le c$, then $a \vee b \le c$; $a \vee b$ is the *least upper bound* of $a$ and $b$.
+     - $a \wedge b \le a$ and $a \wedge b \le b$; $a \wedge b$ is an *lower bound* of $a$ and $b$.
+     - If $c \le a$ and $c \le b$, then $c \le a \wedge b$; $a \wedge b$ is the *greatest lower bound* of $a$ and $b$.
