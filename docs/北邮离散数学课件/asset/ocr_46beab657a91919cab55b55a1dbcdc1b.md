# Some Open Conjectures

+   - **Conjecture:** There are infinitely many primes of the form \(n^2+1\), where \(n\in\mathbb{Z}\).
+   - **Conjecture:** (Twin Prime Conjecture) There are infinitely pairs of primes of the form \((p,p+2)\).
+   - **Conjecture:** (The Hailstone Problem) If \(h(x)=x/2\) when \(x\) is even, and \(3x+1\) when \(x\) is odd, then \(\forall x\in\mathbb{N}\ \exists n\in\mathbb{N}\ h^n(x)=1\) (where the superscript denotes composition of \(h\) with itself \(n\) times).
