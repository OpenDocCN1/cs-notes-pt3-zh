# Converse, Contrapositive, and Inverse
逆命题 逆否命题 否命题

From $p \to q$ we can form new conditional statements.

+   - $q \to p$ is the **converse** of $p \to q$
+   - $\lnot q \to \lnot p$ is the **contrapositive** of $p \to q$
+   - $\lnot p \to \lnot q$ is the **inverse** of $p \to q$

**Example**: Find the converse, inverse, and contrapositive of “It raining is a sufficient condition for my not going to town.”

# **Solution:**

**converse**: If I do not go to town, then it is raining.

**inverse**: If it is not raining, then I will go to town.

**contrapositive**: If I go to town, then it is not raining.
