# Base Conversion

**Example:** Find the octal expansion of \((12345)_{10}\)

**Solution:** Successively dividing by 8 gives:

+   - \(12345 = 8 \cdot 1543 + 1\)
+   - \(1543 = 8 \cdot 192 + 7\)
+   - \(192 = 8 \cdot 24 + 0\)
+   - \(24 = 8 \cdot 3 + 0\)
+   - \(3 = 8 \cdot 0 + 3\)

The remainders are the digits from right to left yielding \((30071)_8\).
