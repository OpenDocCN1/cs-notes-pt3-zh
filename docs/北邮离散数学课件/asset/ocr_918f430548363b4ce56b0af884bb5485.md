# Theorem 2

+   - Let G = (V, E, γ) be a graph with no multiple edges, and let e ∈ E, say e = {a, b}.
+   
+     - G_e : subgraph of G obtained by deleting e,
+   
+     - G^e : the quotient graph of G obtained by merging the end points of e.

+   - Then with x colors:
+   
+     P_G(x) = P_{G_e}(x) - P_{G^e}(x).
