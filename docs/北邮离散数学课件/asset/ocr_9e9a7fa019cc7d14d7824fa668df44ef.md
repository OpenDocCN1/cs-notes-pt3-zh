# # Theorem 5 - Proof

Let \( t_1 \) and \( t_2 \) be any elements of \( T \). There exist \( s_1 \) and \( s_2 \) in \( S \) such that
\( t_1 = f(s_1) \) and \( t_2 = f(s_2) \)

Therefore
\( t_1 * t_2 = f(s_1) * f(s_2) = f(s_1 * s_2) = f(s_2 * s_1) = f(s_2) * f(s_1) = t_2 * t_1 \).

Hence \( (T, *) \) is also commutative.
