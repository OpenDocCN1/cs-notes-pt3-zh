# Prefix(Polish Form)
（前缀表达式（波兰式））

+   - Unambiguously without parentheses
+   - from left to right  Fxy
+   - operation symbol precedes the argument

+   1. x - 64 + 5 ÷ 22
+   2. × 2 + 5 ÷ 22  since the first string of the correct type is - 64 and 6 - 4 = 2
+   3. × 2 + 5 1  replacing ÷ 22 by 2 ÷ 2 or 1
+   4. × 2 6  replacing + 5 1 by 5 + 1 or 6
+   5. 12  replacing × 2 6 by 2 × 6
