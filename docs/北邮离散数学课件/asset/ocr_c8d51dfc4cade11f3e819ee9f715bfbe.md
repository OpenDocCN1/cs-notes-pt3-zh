# Questions for you to think about

+   1. Consider a conjunction \(p_1 \land p_2 \land p_3\)  
+      How many rows are there in its truth table?

+   2. Consider a conjunction  
+      \(p_1 \land p_2 \land \cdots \land p_n\) of \(n\) propositions.  
+      How many rows are there in its truth table?

+   3. Explain why ¬ and ∧ together are sufficient to express *any* Boolean truth table
