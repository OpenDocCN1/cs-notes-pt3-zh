# Transfinite Numbers 超限数

+   - The cardinalities of infinite sets are not natural numbers, but are special objects called *transfinite cardinal numbers*.
+   - The cardinality of the natural numbers, ℵ₀ := |N|, is the *first transfinite cardinal* number. (There are none smaller.)
+   - The *continuum hypothesis* claims that |R| = ℵ₁, the second transfinite cardinal.
