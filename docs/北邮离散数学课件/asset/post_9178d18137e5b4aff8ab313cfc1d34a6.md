
## Cycles

+   For any $n\ge 3$, a *cycle* on $n$ vertices, $C_n$, is a simple graph where $V=\{v_1,v_2,\dots ,v_n\}$ and $E=\{\{v_1,v_2\},\{v_2,v_3\},\dots ,\{v_{n-1},v_n\},\{v_n,v_1\}\}$.

## Wheels

+   - For any \(n\ge 3\), a **wheel** \(W_n\), is a simple graph obtained by taking the cycle \(C_n\) and adding one extra vertex \(v_{\mathrm{hub}}\) and \(n\) extra edges \(\{\{v_{\mathrm{hub}},v_1\},\{v_{\mathrm{hub}},v_2\},\ldots,\{v_{\mathrm{hub}},v_n\}\}\).

## $n$-cubes (hypercubes)

+   - For any $n\in\mathbb{N}$, the hypercube $Q_n$ is a simple graph consisting of two copies of $Q_{n-1}$ connected together at corresponding nodes. $Q_0$ has 1 node.

## n-cubes (hypercubes)

+   - For any $n \in \mathbf{N}$, the hypercube $Q_n$ can be defined recursively as follows:
+     - $Q_0 = \{\{v_0\}, \varnothing\}$ (one node and no edges)
+     - For any $n \in \mathbf{N}$, if $Q_n = (V, E)$, where $V = \{v_1, ..., v_a\}$ and $E = \{e_1, ..., e_b\}$, then $Q_{n+1} = (V \cup \{v_1', ..., v_a'\}, E \cup \{e_1', ..., e_b'\} \cup \{\{v_1, v_1'\}, \{v_2, v_2'\}, ..., \{v_a, v_a'\}\})$ where $v_1', ..., v_a'$ are new vertices, and where if $e_i = \{v_j, v_k\}$ then $e_i' = \{v_j', v_k'\}$.

## Bipartite Graphs

+   - **Def’n.:** A graph $G=(V,E)$ is *bipartite* (two-part) iff $V = V_1 \cup V_2$ where $V_1 \cap V_2=\varnothing$ and $\forall e \in E:$ $\exists v_1 \in V_1, v_2 \in V_2:$ $e=\{v_1,v_2\}$.
+   - **In English:** The graph can be divided into two parts in such a way that all edges go between the two parts.

![](img/c25dfd82fc242c2db53bc942dcad6885_1413_0.png)

This definition can easily be adapted for the case of multigraphs and directed graphs as well.

Can represent with zero-one matrices.

## Complete Bipartite Graphs

+   - For *m,n* ∈ **N**, the *complete bipartite graph* K_{m,n} is a bipartite graph where |V₁| = *m*, |V₂| = *n*, and E = {{v₁,v₂}|v₁ ∈ V₁ ∧ v₂ ∈ V₂}.
+     - That is, there are *m* nodes in the left part, *n* nodes in the right part, and every node in the left part is connected to every node in the right part.

## Subgraphs

+   - A subgraph of a graph $G=(V,E)$ is a graph $H=(W,F)$ where $W\subseteq V$ and $F\subseteq E$.

## Graph Unions

+   - The *union* $G_1 \cup G_2$ of two simple graphs $G_1=(V_1, E_1)$ and $G_2=(V_2,E_2)$ is the simple graph $(V_1 \cup V_2, E_1 \cup E_2)$.

## [定理1 (Handshaking)] 设无向图G=（V，E）有e条边，则G中所有顶点的次之和等于e的两倍。

证明思路：利用数学归纳法。

## [定理2] 无向图中次为奇数的顶点个数恰有偶数个。

证明思路：将图中顶点的次分类，再利用定理1。

## [定理3] 设有向图G=（V，E）有e条边，则G中所有顶点的入次之和等于所有顶点的出次之和，也等于e。

证明思路：利用数学归纳法。

## 有向图与无向图的对应：

有向图对应无向图：去掉箭头。
