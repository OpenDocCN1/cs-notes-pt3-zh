## Theorem 2

Let $R$ be a congruence relation on the group $(G, *)$. Then the semigroup $(G/R, \odot)$ is a group, where the operation $\odot$ is defined on $G/R$ by $[a] \odot [b] = [a*b]$

## Proof

Since a group is a monoid, $G/R$ is a monoid.

Let $[a] \in G/R$.

Then $[a^{-1}] \in G/R$, and $[a] \odot [a^{-1}] = [a*a^{-1}] = [e]$.

So $[a]^{-1} = [a^{-1}]$.

Hence $(G/R, \odot)$ is a group.
