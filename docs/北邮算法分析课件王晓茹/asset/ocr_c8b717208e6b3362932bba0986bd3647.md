# # Flow Shop Scheduling – a simple case with 2 processors

Consider a machine shop that has 2 identical processors _P_<sub>1</sub> and _P_<sub>2</sub>. We have _N_ jobs _J_<sub>1</sub>, ..., _J<sub>N</sub>_ that need to be processed. Each job _J<sub>i</sub>_ consists of two operations _t_<sub>_i_1</sub> and _t_<sub>_i_2</sub>.

A schedule such that _t_<sub>_i_1</sub> precedes _t_<sub>_i_2</sub> on both machines

+   - _j<sub>ij</sub>_ is scheduled on machine _P<sub>j</sub>_

+   - No machine processes more than one job at a time.

+   - _j_<sub>_i_2</sub> may not be scheduled until _j_<sub>_i_1</sub> is finished.

Construct a minimum-completion-time 2 machine schedule for a given set of _N_ jobs.

Let _a<sub>i</sub>_=_t_<sub>_i_1</sub>≠0, and _b<sub>i</sub>_=_t_<sub>_i_2</sub>.

【Example】 Given _N_=4, \(J=\left[\begin{array}{ c c c c }
3 & 4 & 8 & 10 \\
6 & 2 & 9 & 15
\end{array}\right]\) _T_<sub>1234</sub>=40
