| A | B | C | D |

| α | 1 | α | a |
| β | 2 | γ | a |
| γ | 4 | β | b |

| B | D | E |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a | α |
| 3 | a | β |

+   - Natural Join
+     - r ⋈ s

| A | B | C | D | E |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| α | 1 | α | a | γ |
