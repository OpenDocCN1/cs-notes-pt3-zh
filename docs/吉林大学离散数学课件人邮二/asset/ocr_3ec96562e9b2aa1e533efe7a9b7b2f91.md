由定义7.5.1可知，置换即是双射，亦即1-1函数，故$P_\lambda$中的元素满足下列四个性质：

(1)
$(\forall p_1)(\forall p_2)(p_1,p_2 \in P_\lambda \rightarrow p_1 \diamond p_2 \in P_\lambda \land p_2 \diamond p_1 \in P_\lambda)$

(2)
$(\forall p_1)(\forall p_2)(\forall p_3)(p_1,p_2,p_3 \in P_\lambda \rightarrow (p_1 \diamond p_2) \diamond p_3 = p_1 \diamond (p_2 \diamond p_3))$
