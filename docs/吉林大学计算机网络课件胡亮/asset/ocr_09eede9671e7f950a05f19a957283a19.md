# An overview of Computer Network

– The history about internet

| 1966 | Terms, **packets** and **packet switching**, are coined out. A project is funded to create an experimental network. |
| 1969 | FCC decides that the devices not created by AT&T can be attached to its telephone system.
The project evolves into Advanced Research Projects Agency Network(ARPANET).
The first **four** nodes of ARPANET are,
BBN – Bolt Beranek and Newman, a small consulting company
SRI – Stanford Research Institute
The Univ. of California at Santa Barbara
The Univ. of Utah |
