# Understanding Organizations

| Structural frame:
Focuses on roles and responsibilities, coordination, and control. Organization charts help define this frame. | Human resources frame:
Focuses on providing harmony between needs of the organization and needs of people. |
|---|---|
| Political frame:
Assumes organizations are coalitions composed of varied individuals and interest groups. Conflict and power are key issues. | Symbolic frame: Focuses on symbols and meanings related to events. Culture is important. |
