# # 基本的等价公式

\( E_{21} \quad P \wedge F \iff F \quad \left\{ \right. 零律 \)

\( E_{22} \quad P \lor T \iff T \quad \right\} \)

\( E_{24} \quad P \iff T \iff P \)

\( E_{25} \quad P \iff F \iff \neg P \)

\( E_{26} \quad P \iff Q \iff (P \to Q) \land (Q \to P) \iff (P \land Q) \lor (\neg P \land \neg Q) \)

\( E_{27} \quad P \to Q \iff \neg P \lor Q \)

\( E_{28} \quad P \land Q \to R \iff (P \to (Q \to R)) \quad 输出律 \)

\( E_{29} \quad P \land (P \lor Q) \iff P \quad \left\{ \right. 吸收律 \)

\( E_{30} \quad P \lor (P \land Q) \iff P \quad \right\} \)
