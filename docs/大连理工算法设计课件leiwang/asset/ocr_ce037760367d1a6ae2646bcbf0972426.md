# # Multiplication method example

$h(k) = (A \cdot k \bmod 2^w) \text{ rsh } (w - r)$

Suppose that \( m = 8 = 2^3 \) and that our computer has \( w = 7 \)-bit words:

\(\begin{array}{r}
1011001 \quad = A \\
\times \\
1101011 \quad = k \\
\hline
10010100110011 \\
\underbrace{1001}_{h(k)}
\end{array}\)
