
## 3.2 The Interpolation Method of Generating Training Samples

For the terrain semantic segmentation, large amounts of samples with dense annotations are necessary. Sufficient diverse samples can be synthesized and automatically labeled through the virtual world. However, the domain gap between virtual and real world leads to accuracy decrease. Hence, the interpolated samples of virtual and real images contribute to training, and each sample has a one-dimensional style vector to specify the style of the image. The style codes of the real and virtual worlds are manually identified, and those of generated interpolated images are generated by the style vector interpolation.

Specifically, the generator takes as inputs the style code vector of the sample and the semantic layout of anthropocentric scene, then generates the corresponding synthetic image. All training samples are composed of synthetic images (interpolated between virtual and real) and corresponding auxiliary inputs (layout and style vector).

It is worth mentioning that not all the generated interpolated images are of high quality, some have low-level artifacts. Thus three terms of constraints are applied to select images with clear structures for training:

- 1. **Structural similarity.** We apply the similarity between the semantic layout and the generated image by a pre-trained segmentation network. Only the images with high confidence are selected.
- 2. **FID's quality assessment.** We use the Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) to measure the quality of the whole generated image set.
- 3. **Local inconsistency suppression.** We suppress the low-level artifacts by a local texture comparison using the structural similarity (SSIM) between the generated and real images.

Finally, the training set is constructed with the collected interpolated images (the distribution shown in Fig. 4), and each image is assigned with its corresponding style vector and semantic layout.

The efficacy of the proposed auxiliary inputs (semantic layout and style vector) can be evaluated by comparing different training schemes, and the comparison results are shown in Table 1. Combining both style vector and semantic layout as auxiliary inputs yields the best accuracy.

As shown in Table 1, when the style vector is used as an auxiliary input, the mIoU is improved from 61.2% to 68.7%; when additionally the semantic layout is used, the mIoU is further improved to 71.6%. The results verify the effectiveness of the two auxiliary inputs.

## 7.1 编码

### 7.1.1 选择程序设计语言

- 程序设计语言是人和计算机通信的最基本的工具
- 影响人的思维和解题方式
- 影响人和计算机通信的方式和质量
- 影响其他人阅读和理解程序的难易程度

选择一种适当的程序设计语言显得尤为重要

- **汇编语言**应仅在以下特殊应用领域中使用
  - 对程序执行时间和使用的空间都有很严格限制的情况
  - 需要产生任意的甚至非法的指令序列
  - 体系结构特殊的微处理机，以致在这类机器上通常不能实现高级语言编译程序
  - 或者大型系统中执行时间非常关键的（或直接依赖于硬件的）一小部分代码
- 其他程序应该一律用**高级语言**书写

### 选择程序设计语言应考虑的因素

- 1）软件的应用领域
- 2）算法和数据结构的复杂性
- 3）系统用户的要求
- 4）软件运行环境
- 5）软件开发的方法
- 6）可得到的软件工具
- 7）工程规模
- 8）软件的可移植性要求
- 9）程序员的知识水平

### 7.1.2 编码风格

- 程序实际上也是一种**供人阅读的文章**，有一个文章的风格问题
  - 应该使程序具有良好的风格
  - 力图从编码原则的角度提高程序的可读性，改善程序质量
- 编程风格包括以下五个方面
  - 源程序文档化
  - 数据说明
  - 语句构造
  - 输入输出
  - 效率

### 数据说明(Data Description)

- 在设计阶段已经确定了数据结构的组织及其复杂性。在编写程序时，则需要注意数据说明的风格
- 为了使程序中数据说明更易于理解和维护，必须注意以下几点
  - 1.数据说明的次序应当规范化
  - 2.说明语句中变量安排有序化
  - 3.使用注释说明复杂数据结构

### 语句构造(Program Structure)

- 在设计阶段确定了软件的逻辑流结构，但构造单个语句则是编码阶段的任务
- 语句构造应力求简单，直接，**不能为了片面追求效率而使语句复杂化**
- 常用规则
  - 一行内只写一条语句
  - 首先考虑清晰性
  - 直截了当说明程序员意图
  - 其他规则
