# # 2D Transformation

# ### 3.Scale

+   - Scaling is an non-rigid-body transformation

\(x'=x \cdot s_x,\quad y'=y \cdot s_y\)

Then

\(\begin{bmatrix} x' \\ y' \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} s_x & 0 \\ 0 & s_y \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix}\)
