![](img/258e4144ed4b66c8fd4b0f298118df23_153_0.png)

- Each object is defined in The Model Coordinate System
- No information about where the object is placed
- It is possible to reuse model coordinate, since they carry no information of relationships between different objects

### 2D Transformation

#### 1. Translation

- Translation is an operation that displace points by a fixed distance in a given direction.

Move point P(x,y) to P'(x',y')

\(x' = x + \Delta x, y' = y + \Delta y\)

Then

\(\begin{bmatrix} x' \\ y' \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} \Delta x \\ \Delta y \end{bmatrix}\)

![](img/258e4144ed4b66c8fd4b0f298118df23_161_0.png)

### 2D Transformation

#### 2. Rotation

Rotate (x,y), angle θ,based on origin point

\(x'=r\cos(\theta+\phi)=r\cos\phi\cos\theta-r\sin\phi\sin\theta\)
\(y'=r\sin(\theta+\phi)=r\cos\phi\sin\theta+r\sin\phi\cos\theta\)

\(\therefore x=r\cos\phi,y=r\sin\phi\)

\(\therefore x'=x\cos\theta-y\sin\theta,y'=x\sin\theta+y\cos\theta\)

### Then

\(\left[\begin{array}{ c }
x' \\
y'
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{ c c }
\cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\
\sin\theta & \cos\theta
\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{ c }
x \\
y
\end{array}\right]\)

![](img/258e4144ed4b66c8fd4b0f298118df23_162_0.png)

### 2D Transformation

#### 3.Scale

- Scaling is an non-rigid-body transformation

\(x'=x \cdot s_x,\quad y'=y \cdot s_y\)

Then

\(\begin{bmatrix} x' \\ y' \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} s_x & 0 \\ 0 & s_y \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix}\)

![](img/258e4144ed4b66c8fd4b0f298118df23_163_0.png)

### 3D Translation

- Translation is an operation that displace points by a fixed distance in a given direction.

$$\begin{bmatrix}x'\\y'\\z'\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\\z\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}\Delta x\\\Delta y\\\Delta z\end{bmatrix}$$

That is:

$$\begin{bmatrix}x'\\y'\\z'\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\\z\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}\Delta x\\\Delta y\\\Delta z\end{bmatrix}$$

![](img/258e4144ed4b66c8fd4b0f298118df23_164_0.png)

### 3D Rotation

- For a point \( (x, y, z) = (r \cos \varphi, r \sin \varphi, z) \) the equations for rotation about the z axis by an angle \( \alpha \) are:

\( x' = r \cos(\varphi + \alpha) = x \cos \alpha - y \sin \alpha \)
\( y' = r \sin(\varphi + \alpha) = x \sin \alpha + y \cos \alpha \)
\( z' = z \)

\[ \begin{bmatrix} x' \\ y' \\ z' \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos \alpha & -\sin \alpha & 0 \\ \sin \alpha & \cos \alpha & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{bmatrix} \]

- The matrices for rotation about the y and x axis are:

\[ \begin{bmatrix} \sin \alpha & 0 & \cos \alpha \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ \cos \alpha & 0 & -\sin \alpha \end{bmatrix} \quad , \quad \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \cos \alpha & -\sin \alpha \\ 0 & \sin \alpha & \cos \alpha \end{bmatrix} \]
