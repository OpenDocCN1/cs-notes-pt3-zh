# B-spline surface

(n+1) × (m+1) control points: $P_{i,j}$  
degrees of $u$, $v$: $p$, $q$;  
Nodes: $U=[u_0,u_1,\ldots,u_{n+p+1}]$, $V=[v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_{m+q+1}]$, Then a tensor B-spline surface with degree $p\times q$:

$$S(u,v)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{m}N_{i,p}(u)N_{j,q}(v)P_{i,j}$$
