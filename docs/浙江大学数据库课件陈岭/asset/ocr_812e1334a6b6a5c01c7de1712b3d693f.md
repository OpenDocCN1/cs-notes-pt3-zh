# # Example of Non Lossless-Join Decomposition

# ## Decomposition of R = (A, B)

R₁ = (A), R₂ = (B)

E.g.:

r = | A | B |
|---|---|
| α | 1 |
| α | 2 |
| β | 1 |

Πₐ(r) = | A |
|---|
| α |
| β |

Πᵦ(r) = | B |
|---|
| 1 |
| 2 |

Πₐ (r) ⋈ Πᵦ (r) = | A | B |
|---|---|
| α | 1 |
| α | 2 |
| β | 1 |
| β | 2 | ≠ r

# ## A bad decomposition !

> It's not lossless-join decomposition. It's illegal !
