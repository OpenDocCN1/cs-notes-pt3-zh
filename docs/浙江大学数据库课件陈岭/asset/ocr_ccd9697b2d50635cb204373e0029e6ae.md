# # Example2:

A company database needs to store information about **employees** (identified by **empid**, with **name**, **salary** and **phone** as attributes); **departments** (identified by **dno**, with **dname** and **budget** as attributes) and **children** of the empoyee (with **name** and **age** as attributes). Employees **work** in departments; each department is **managed** by an employee; a child must be identified uniquely by **name** when **parent** (who is an employee; assume that only _one parent_ works for the company) is **known**. We are not interested in information about a child once the parent leaves the company. Draw an E-R diagram that captures this information, and then transform it into relational schema.
