# # Relations关系

解：
\( R = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \ 0 & 0 & 1 \ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad R^2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \ 0 & 0 & 0 \ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} = R^3 \square \)

故
\( R^* = R \cup R^2 \cup R^3 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \ 0 & 0 & 1 \ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \)

\( R^* = \{ (1, 1), (1, 2), (1, 3), (2, 3) \} \)
