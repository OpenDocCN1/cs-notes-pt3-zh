# Example 4

Suppose that a connected planar simple graph has 20 vertices, each of degree 3.Into how many regions does a representation of this planar graph split the plane ?

Solution : This graph has 20 vertices ,each of degree 3,so that v=20 . Since the sum of the degrees of the vertices, 3v =3·20=60,is equal to twice the number of edges, 2e, we have 2e= 60,or e = 30. Consequently,from Euler’s formula, the number of regions is

r = e –v +2 = 30-20+2= 12.
