例1：A={1, 2, 3, 4}，I为整数集

R={(a, b) | a, b∈A, \(\frac{a-b}{2} \in I\)} □

S={(a, b) | a, b∈A, \(\frac{a-b}{3} \in I\), a-b>0}

求：R∪S, R∩S, R̄, R-S, S-R, S⊕R ■

解：R与S都是A上的二元关系

R={(1, 1), (2, 2), (3, 3), (4, 4), (1, 3), (3, 1), (2, 4), (4, 2)}

S={(4, 1)}

R∪S={(1, 1), (2, 2), (3, 3), (4, 4), (1, 3), (3, 1), (2, 4), (4, 2), (4, 1)}
