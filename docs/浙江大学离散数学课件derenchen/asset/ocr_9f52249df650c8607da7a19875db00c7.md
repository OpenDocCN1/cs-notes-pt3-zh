# # Lemma 1

If a,b,and c are positive integers such that gcd(a,b)=1 and a | bc,then a | c.

Proof : since gcd (a,b)=1,by Theorem 1 there are integers s and t such that sa+tb=1. Multiplying both sides of this equation by c, we obtain sac+tbc=c, Using Theorem 1 of Section 2.3,we can use this last equation to show that a | c. By part 2 of that theorem, a | tbc.Since a | sac and a | tbc,by part 1 of that theorem,we conclude that a divides sac+tbc,and a | c.This finishes the proof.
