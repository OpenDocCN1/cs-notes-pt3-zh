# Example 4

Find the base 8 expansion of (12345 )10 .

_Solution:_ First,divide 12345 by 8 to obtain

12345=8·1543+1.

Successively dividing quotients by 8 gives

1543=8·192+7

192=8·24+0

24=8·3+0

3=8·0+3.

Since the remainders are the digits of the base 8
expansion of 12345,it follows that

(12345 )10 =(30071 )8 .
