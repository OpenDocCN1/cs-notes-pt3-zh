# 定理7.5 设F为关系，A, B为集合，则

(1) \( F \cap (A \cup B) = F \cap A \cup F \cap B \);

(2) \( F[A \cup B] = F[A] \cup F[B] \)

(3) \( F \cap (A \cap B) = F \cap A \cap F \cap B \);

(4) \( F[A \cap B] = F[A] \cap F[B] \)

证：以(1)和(4)为例

(1) \( <x,y> \in F \cap (A \cup B) \Leftrightarrow <x,y> \in F \land x \in (A \cup B) \)

\( \Leftrightarrow <x,y> \in F \land (x \in A \lor x \in B) \)

\( \Leftrightarrow (<x,y> \in F \land x \in A) \lor (<x,y> \in F \land x \in B) \)

\( \Leftrightarrow <x,y> \in F \cap A \lor <x,y> \in F \cap B \)

\( \Leftrightarrow <x,y> \in (F \cap A \cup F \cap B) \)

\(\therefore F \cap (A \cup B) = F \cap A \cup F \cap B\)
