$a_0+b_0+c_0=c_1\cdot2+s_1,$

Where $s_1$ is the next bit (from the right) in the binary expansion of $a+b$, and $c_1$ is the carry. Continue this process, adding the corresponding bits in the two binary expansion of $a+b$. At the last stage, add $a_{n-1}$, $b_{n-1}$, and $c_{n-2}$ to obtain $c_{n-1}\cdot2+s_{n-1}$. The leading bit of the sum is $s_n=c_{n-1}$. This procedure produces the binary expansion of the sum, namely, $a+b=(s_ns_{n-1}\cdots s_1s_0)_2$.
