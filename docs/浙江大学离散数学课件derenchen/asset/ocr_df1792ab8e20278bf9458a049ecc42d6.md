# # Example 6 Find the prime factorization of 7007.

Solution: first perform divisions of 7007 by successive
primes,beginning with 2. None of 2,3 and 5 divides 7007. However,7
divides 7, with 7007/7=1001. Next,divide 1001 by successive prime
beginning with 7, 7 also divides 1001,since 1001/7 =143 . Continue by
dividing 143 by successive prime beginning with 7, 7 does not divide
143, 11 does divide 143,143/11=13, 13 is a prime so

7007=7×7 × 11 × 13=7 ²× 11 × 13
