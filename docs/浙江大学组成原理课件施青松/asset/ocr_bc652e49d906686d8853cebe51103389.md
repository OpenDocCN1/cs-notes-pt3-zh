# # Overflow conditions

# ## General overflow conditions

| Operation | Operand A | Operand B | Result overflow |
|---|---|---|---|
| A+B | ≥0 | ≥0 | <0 _(01)_ |
| A+B | <0 | <0 | ≥0 _(10)_ |
| A-B | ≥0 | <0 | <0 _(01)_ |
| A-B | <0 | ≥0 | ≥0 _(10)_ |
