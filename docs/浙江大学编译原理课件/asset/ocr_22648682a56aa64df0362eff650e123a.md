# 4.3.2 Follow Sets

# Example: the simple expression grammar.

(1) exp → exp addop term
(2) exp → term
(3) addop → +
(4) addop → -
(5) term → term mulop factor
(6) term → factor
(7) mulop → *
(8) factor →(exp )
(9) factor →number

The First sets:
First(exp)={(,number}
First(term)={(,number}
First(factor)={(,number}
First(addop)={+,-}
First(mulop)={*}

The Follow sets:
Follow (exp)={ $,+,-,) }
Follow (addop)={(,number}
Follow (term)={$,+,-,* ,)}
Follow (mulop)={(,number}
Follow (factor)={$,+,-,* ,)}
