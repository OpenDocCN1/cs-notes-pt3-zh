# 4.2.3 left Recursion Removal and Left Factoring

The grammar with its left recursion removed would give rise to the procedures exp and exp’ as follows:

exp → term exp’
exp’→ addop term exp’ | ε
addop → + | -
term → factor term’
term’ → mulop factor term’ | ε
mulop → *
factor →(expr) | number

exp → term exp’
Procedure exp
Begin
Term;
Exp’;
End exp;

exp’→ addop term exp’ | ε
Procedure exp’
Begin
Case token of
+: match(+);
term;
exp’;
-: match(-);
term;
exp’;
end case;
end exp’
