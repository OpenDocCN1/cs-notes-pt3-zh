# 4.2.3 left Recursion Removal and Left Factoring

+   • CASE 1: Simple immediate left recursion

A → A α | β

We rewrite this grammar rule into two rules:

A → βA′
A′ → α A′ | ε

+   • EXAMPLE:

exp → exp addop term | term
exp → term exp′
exp′ → addop term exp′ | ε
